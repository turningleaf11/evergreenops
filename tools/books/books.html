How categories are decided. Paired transfers between your own accounts are matched by counterparty account number and removed from the profit & loss automatically. Movements between two different entities are held as intercompany — excluded from the P&L but not discarded, since they need due-to/due-from entries on both balance sheets. Known payees post by rule. Everything else is flagged for you.

Anything involving a source document — mortgage principal versus interest, property sale proceeds, earnest money, cross-entity misposts — is deliberately never auto-assigned. A statement with no review list is a statement that guessed.

Change any category with the dropdown in its row; your overrides are saved in this browser and survive a reload. This is a working draft, not a filing. Have a licensed preparer review it.
